About DAMAC Islands 2.

DAMAC Islands 2 is an approximately 20-million-sq-ft island-inspired master community in Dubailand, Dubai - eight themed clusters (Bahamas, Tahiti, Bermuda, Barbados, Cuba, Maui, Mauritius and Antigua) built around crystal lagoons, sandy beaches and resort-style amenities.

Every cluster combines nature, wellness, adventure, social and cultural experiences - from floating cinemas and wellness pavilions to jungle adventure trails and family-friendly lagoon beaches.
